Where should models be saved?

Knull & Clear stores TTS weights only on your PC — nothing is downloaded until you pick a folder. Choose a location for your model library (for example a folder named knull-models).

  • Models save as {folder}/models/… on your PC
  • You can change the folder later under Storage
  • Chrome or Edge recommended (folder access API)
